Amanda.

Prizes lie in a row. Two girls take turns grabbing 1 to 6 from the far end. The only thing Amanda wants is the book of fishes — and it lies first, so it is taken last. She can pick who starts. Can she always get it?

The answer

Every single n.

Amanda can force the book for any number of prizes, from one to a billion. The key is the number 7 — one more than the largest take.

n ≡ 0 (mod 7): let Bodil start  ·  otherwise start, take n mod 7

After that, whatever Bodil takes (k), Amanda takes 7 − k. Every pair of moves removes exactly seven prizes, so Amanda always leaves a multiple of 7 behind. When the line gets short, it's Bodil who faces the full seven.

Traps in the first 98 positions

Teal squares are multiples of 7 — whoever must move from there loses the book. Everything else is a win for the player to move.
