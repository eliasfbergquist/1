What to look for

  • The ring in the middle shows the remaining prizes mod 7. A glowing 0 means a trap.
  • The line is drawn in groups of seven from the book. A full last group is a trap, a partial one tells you how many to take.
  • The star ★ on the start buttons shows Amanda's best opener.
  • Turn off the winning-take hint and see if you can still spot it.
