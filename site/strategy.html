Exactly what Amanda does

One number: seven.

The largest take is 6, so every pair of moves (one by each player) can be made to remove exactly 6 + 1 = 7 prizes. Amanda's whole job is to keep the remaining count a multiple of 7 after each of her turns.

Common mistakes

🙅

Starting when n is a multiple of 7

You hand Bodil the whole plan. With 7, 14, 21… the first player is the one in trouble.

🧮

Taking the wrong remainder

The opening take is n mod 7, not n − 7. For n = 20 that is 6, leaving 14.

🏁

Forgetting to finish

If 6 or fewer remain on your turn, take them all. The "7 − k" rule is for when more remain than you can take.
