Amanda's decision

Choose the line.

You see how many prizes there are, then you decide who starts. Pick wisely — then play it out, in person.

See the strategy
🎯

Take from here to the end

Point at any prize within the last six. Every prize from it to the end lights up gold: that's your take. Click to grab them all.

🟦

The teal bars are traps

The glowing bars on the table mark positions 7, 14, 21… If you leave the end of the line sitting exactly on a bar after your move, you've set the trap.

✨

The gold glow is the answer

With hints on, the winning take pulses on the line. Switch hints off and try to find it by reading the bars alone.

🔫

The UMP (optional chaos)

Press F to pull out a cartoon foam-dart UMP. Shoot your opponent and a wave of seven copies pops out of the playground, followed by a second wave with a big one. Hold click to fire, aim for heads, and pop them all before they reach you.

⏸️

The prize game waits

While the swarm is around, nobody takes prizes. Pop every copy and the real player peeks out and the game carries on. If they overwhelm you, they scatter and you pick up where you left off. No penalty, just pride.

Controls

W A S DWalk around the schoolyard (Shift to sprint). Arrow keys turn the view if pointer lock is unavailable.
MouseLook around after clicking the view once. Press Esc to release the mouse. You can also drag to look.
ClickTake the prize you're pointing at, plus every prize behind it to the end of the line (max 6).
1–6Shortcut: take exactly that many from the end. Also available as the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
Space / BSpace glides you back to the end of the line. B turns you to look at the book.
FEquip or holster the UMP (a cartoon foam-dart blaster). With it out, clicking fires instead of taking prizes.
Hold click · RFull-auto fire (30-round magazine, infinite reserve). R reloads. Headshots count double.
H MToggle hints, toggle sound.
