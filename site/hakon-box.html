The fight plan

  1. Read the tell.

    An arrow over his head shows which glove is coming. A left hook comes from the left, so lean right. A centre jab? Lean either way, or block.

  2. Count to three.

    Håkon throws exactly three punches per combo. After the third, he is out of breath: the OPEN window. Hits there do extra damage.

  3. Hit him while he winds up.

    A well-timed punch during a wind-up interrupts the whole combo.

  4. Seven is the magic number.

    Every landed hit fills a pip. The seventh hit is The Seven: big damage and a long daze (hits on a dazed Håkon don't fill pips, so no endless chain). But when he hits you, you lose 3 pips. Of course he takes 3.

Why 7 pips?

Mod 7, in the ring

Same maths, different gloves: in the prize game Amanda wins by keeping the count at a multiple of 7. Here your pips run 0 → 7 and then reset to 0. Get hit and Håkon's “3” pushes you back, exactly as in the strategy page.

Win on knockout, or on points after round 3 (more health left).
