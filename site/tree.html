The game as a graph.

Every position is a dot (the number of prizes left). Every legal take is an arc from a bigger dot to a smaller one. Gold arcs lead into traps: those are the winning moves. Isolate them and a very simple skeleton appears.

The skeleton

Switch to Winning only. Every non-trap dot has exactly one gold arc, going down to the next lower multiple of m + 1. Every trap dot has none. Following gold arcs from anywhere lands you on a trap, and then it is the opponent who has no gold move.

That skeleton is Amanda's strategy, drawn: when it's your turn on a gold-arc dot, follow the arc.
