Positions 1 to 126. Traps in teal, wins in dim amber dots. Six wins, one trap, six wins, one trap…

Grid, width 7

With seven columns the traps stack into one clean vertical stripe: the last column. Slide the width to 6 or 8 and the stripe leans.

🌀

Spiral

On a square spiral the traps trace diagonals that bend because 7 doesn't divide the side lengths. Pretty, but not as clear as the grid.

☀️

Spokes

Seven rays from the centre; every multiple of 7 sits on the same ray. Set the period to 7 and watch the traps form a single spoke.
